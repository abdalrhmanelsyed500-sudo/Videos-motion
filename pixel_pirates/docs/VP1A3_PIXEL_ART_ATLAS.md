# VP-1A.3 — The authored pixel-art atlas of the ship (real game integration)

Branch `arena/7464ca7a-videos-motion`. Renderer: Compatibility "Authored HD-2D". Baseline of this step: VP-1A.2 (`cebbe4d`).
This step answers the standing correction of 2026-10-21: *keep all programming and systems, but the surfaces must be
redrawn as rich authored pixel art integrated with the 3D models* — individual planks, grain, nails, strakes, weave and
plate drawn as pixel clusters, not shader maths. Shader-only upgrades were explicitly rejected; here the **drawn art does
the visual work** and the VP-1A.2 lighting (sun + shadows, lanterns, AO, ocean model) lights it.

## 1. What was built

### 1.1 The atlas — `game/art/vp1/ship_px.png` (1024², generated by `tools/vp1/make_px_atlas.py`)

Sixteen seamless 256 px tiles in a 4×4 grid, one per material, each **drawn** (numpy as a pixel-art brush, not a filter):
a 6–7 colour ramp per tile with values quantised into crisp clusters, periodic (wrapped) value-noise so every tile tiles
perfectly, Bayer-matrix dithering for wear and stains, and hand-placed pixel features. Tiles:

| # | material | drawn features | px/m |
|---|----------|----------------|------|
| 0 | deck | 16 px planks, per-plank hue+value, 2 px seams with lit lip, butt joints, 2×2 nail heads with highlight, grain strokes, cracks, dithered wear and stains | 48 |
| 1 | hull | 12 px strakes (clinker laps: 2 px shadow + lit lip), per-strake value, rivets, streaking | 40 |
| 2 | timber | beams/masts: straight grain, knots, glue lines, lit/shadow edges | 40 |
| 3 | barrel staves | curved staves with gaps and crowns, hoop line (hoops themselves are iron in the shader at real heights) | 32 |
| 4 | canvas | weave checker, 3 px seam rows with stitches, grommets, a sewn patch (tile carries its own colour) | 64 |
| 5 | rope | diagonal 3-strand twist with fibre noise and frays | 48 |
| 6 | iron | plate ramps, scratch highlights, rust blooms, rivet heads with shadow | 48 |
| 7 | crate | boards, iron nails, scuffs | 40 |
| 8 | painted | brush strokes, chips down to wood | 40 |
| 9 | net | twine lattice with knots (tile carries its own colour; shader discards the holes) | 48 |
| 10 | lamp | glass glow gradient, bubbles, soot at the top (own colour) | 64 |
| 11 | flag | dark weave, frayed edge | 40 |
| 12 | sand | grains, speckles, wind ripples (reserved for Anvil Cay) | 48 |
| 13 | grass | blades with lit tips, flowers (reserved) | 48 |
| 14 | cobble | Voronoi-ish stones with mortar and moss (reserved) | 40 |
| 15 | rock | strata, lit faces (reserved) | 40 |

A final per-tile "grit" pass adds single-pixel highlights/shadows so no surface is perfectly clean. The deck tile is
transposed so planks run fore-aft in ship space.

### 1.2 Mesh UVs — `game/h2d/ship_mesh.gd` (systems untouched)

- `PX_DENS` (px per metre per tile), `PX_TILE`, `PX_INSET`; helpers `px_origin`, `px_uv2` (pattern metres → atlas uv, wrapping
  inside one tile with an inset so mipmaps never bleed), `px_uv` (picks the face plane: floors x/z, walls the in-plane axes).
- **Every** primitive (`quad`, `quad_c`, `tri`, `lathe`, `surface`) now emits real atlas UVs in `ARRAY_TEX_UV`: planar
  projection for boxes/walls, arc-length × height around lathes (barrels, masts, lanterns), parametric uvscale for sails.
- `ARRAY_TEX_UV2` now carries `(material id, relative height)` for the shader's per-material logic (barrel hoops, sack ties,
  hero dissolve cap) — previously that lived in TEX_UV, which is now the art.

### 1.3 Shader — `game/h2d/shaders/ship_deck.gdshader`

- `uniform sampler2D pxatlas : filter_nearest_mipmap, repeat_disable` (hint spelling verified against Godot 4.7 source);
  the fragment reads the authored pixel at the mesh UV with nearest+mips (crisp when close, no shimmer on the Dev-Lite
  internal resolution). The VP-1A channel atlas (`mats`, `mat_tile`, `tile_m`) is gone.
- Colour policy: materials with their own authored colour (canvas 4, lamp 10/12, net 14) use the tile as-is; every other
  material multiplies the tile by its **grey value tint ×2** (so per-object lightness/variation still works and the drawn
  hue+contrast of the art survives).
- Kept procedural by design: fish scales and sack cloth (tiny props, patterns read better), barrel hoops as iron bands at
  their real heights over the stave tile, net hole discard. VP-1A.2 lighting (sun shadows, lanterns, AO, contact, glints)
  is unchanged and now lights drawn pixels.
- `tex_on = 0` remains a flat-tint fallback if the atlas file is ever missing.

### 1.4 View — `game/h2d/ship_deck_view.gd`

- `MATS_PATH` → `res://art/vp1/ship_px.png`; the texture is imported if present else loaded from file with
  `generate_mipmaps()` (needed for the Dev-Lite 360p internal buffer).
- Object tints converted to **grey value tints** (deck .53, hull .44, wood .36/.50/.63, trim .55/.50/.48, cabin .50,
  roof .40, crate .50) so they modulate the art instead of painting over it; deliberate colour kept only where it is
  information: rope, iron, brass telescope, cargo-crate owner tints, fish/sack tints, skylight glass.
- Sail surfaces get atlas uvscales (main 7×9 m, jib 6×7 m) so the weave is at canvas density.

## 2. How to test (Windows, from the ZIP)

Same as VP-1A.2 plus: 1) launch, F5 at the Capital Port, walk to the dock, `E` aboard; 2) look at the deck in daylight:
plank rows with seams, nails and butt joints must be visible at normal play distance; 3) the hull from the pier shows
strake laps; barrels show staves + iron hoops; the sail shows weave, seam rows and the patch; 4) F1 to Low-End: art stays
readable (softer, mips); F2/F3/F4 cycle times of day — the drawn art is lit, never flat; 5) full deck walk (helm → barrels
→ winch → cabin → ladder → hold) — no surface loses its art, nothing blocks play or fishing. Harness keys unchanged
(Q/U/I/O/J/K/L/H/V + Backslash place mid-deck, Z, F9/F10/F11). Suite: `bash tools/godot_node/run_suite.sh`.

## 4. Known limits (honest)

1. Dev-Lite (360p internal) softens the art through mipmaps — intended trade for shimmer-free downscale; crisp at 720p+.
2. Deck tile wraps every 256/48 ≈ 5.33 m, so a long deck repeats its tone pattern once; seams stay continuous. A per-object
   UV offset or a 512 px deck tile can break the repetition later (VP-1C candidate).
3. The cabin roof quad reads as a smooth canvas-covered top at some angles (its planar UV lands on worn plank centres);
   acceptable as a canvased roof, revisit in VP-1C if objectionable.
4. Hull sides show vertical plank columns from the planar projection; strake laps read on the bow/stern curves.
5. Intel HD 3000 performance remains **UNMEASURED** (no such hardware here); the Low-End profile is the safe setting there.

## 5. CANON update

- Ship surfaces = authored atlas `game/art/vp1/ship_px.png`, 16 tiles, densities `PX_DENS` in `ship_mesh.gd`
  (deck 48 px/m ⇒ 0.34 m planks; gameplay deck density stays 14.4 px/m, art density is per-metre texels).
- TEX_UV = atlas art; TEX_UV2 = (material, relative height). Object tints are grey value tints; colour = information only.
- Tiles 12–15 (sand/grass/cobble/rock) are reserved for VP-1B Anvil Cay — same pipeline, no new tech.
- Everything from VP-1A/VP-1A.2 canon stands (lighting, ocean, props, profiles, suite).
