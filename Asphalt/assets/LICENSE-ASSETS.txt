All generated textures, portraits, UI art, data and WAV audio in this project are original works created by tools/generate_assets.py for THE ASPHALT GROOMS. Arabic and Latin title/signage text is rasterized with DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu license); no third-party game assets, music, photographs, or commercial model libraries are included. Audio files are procedural placeholders. The three images in docs/reference are user-provided visual references and are not used as game textures.
